# 参考资料与证据边界

本知识库由项目整理、概括和配方创作组成，不照搬教材长篇原文。**和弦间隔、功能属于乐理知识；“情感/场景”属于风格化的审美描述，不是确定的心理效应。**

1. Open Music Theory — [Triads and seventh chords](https://open-musictheory.github.io/docs/fundamentals/triads/). 三和弦、七和弦、缩写、罗马数字等。
2. Open Music Theory — [Harmonic functions](https://open-musictheory.github.io/docs/harmony/harmonicFunctions/). 主/前属/属功能解释。
3. Open Music Theory — [Modal mixture](https://quadriviumpress.com/musicTheory/ch-044-modal-mixture/). 平行调借用和弦与不等同于转调。
4. University of Puget Sound — [Adding Non-Chord Tones](https://musictheory.pugetsound.edu/mt21c/AddingNonChordTones.html). 旋律中经过音、邻音、挂留及处理。
5. Open Music Theory — [Embellishing tones](https://open-musictheory.github.io/docs/voice-leading-and-model-composition/strict-four-voice-composition/embellishingTones/). 强弱拍、经过音、邻音基本概念。
6. Oolimo — [Sus & Add Chords](https://www.oolimo.com/en/chord-types/sus-add). sus 与 add 的定义与实例。
7. Harmony and Musicianship with Solfège — [Development of 9th, 11th, 13th Chords](https://pressbooks.pub/harmonyandmusicianshipwithsolfege/chapter/the-development-of-9th-11th-and-13th-chords/). 延伸和弦层叠原则。
8. Zhang et al. (2025), *Moderating effects of chord progressions on the emotional experience of major and minor chords*, Acta Psychologica. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39793277/). 研究显示情绪评价受和弦进行与终止稳定性调节。
9. Labbé et al. (2020), *Affective experiences to chords are modulated by mode, meter, tempo, and subjective entrainment*. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/0305735620906887). 调式、节拍、速度影响对和弦的情绪体验。
10. OpenAI — [Build skills](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/build-skills). 项目级 Codex Skill 的目录与元数据约定。

**术语说明：**美式和弦符号 `B`=B natural, `B♭`=B-flat；中文简谱的 `7` 对应哪一音高，取决于 `1=...` 调号。所有 C 根音示例都需依实际调号移调，`♭VI` 等罗马数字相对于**当前**主音。
